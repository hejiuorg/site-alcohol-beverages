GB/T 10781.1-2021 Quality requirements for baijiu — Part 1: Nongxiangxing baijiu

The current Chinese national standard for Nongxiangxing (strong-aroma) baijiu, published on 2021-03-09 and effective from 2022-04-01; it fully replaces GB/T 10781.1-2006 and forms Part 1 of the "Quality requirements for baijiu" series.

GB/T 10781.1-2021 is the current Chinese national standard for Nongxiangxing, or strong-aroma, baijiu. It forms Part 1 of the "Quality requirements for baijiu" series and sets out terminology and definitions, product classification, requirements, test methods and inspection rules, together with marking, packaging, transport and storage. It answers the question "what may be called Nongxiangxing baijiu, and what basic requirements must it meet"; it does not grade an individual bottle for the consumer.

The 2006 edition was titled Nongxiangxing baijiu. The 2021 edition turns it into Part 1 of the "Quality requirements for baijiu" series. The change is more than a wording adjustment: it places Nongxiangxing baijiu in a unified series framework, so later parts for Jiangxiangxing, Qingxiangxing and other aroma types can follow the same logic. Cite the full code "GB/T 10781.1-2021" rather than "GB/T 10781.1", otherwise the 2006 edition may be confused with the current one.

What the standard covers

The standard addresses the product quality of Nongxiangxing baijiu. It first gives terminology and definitions to establish what "Nongxiangxing baijiu" means; it then divides products into types and sets sensory, physicochemical and other requirements for each; it goes on to specify test methods, sampling and judgement rules, and finally covers labelling, packaging, transport and storage. In other words, it is not merely a definition: it links naming, classification, testing and labelling into one chain.

The standard is a recommended national standard (GB/T), not a food-safety national standard. It sets product-quality requirements, while food safety, contaminant limits and the use of food additives are governed by the relevant food-safety standards and regulations. The two cannot replace each other: meeting a product standard does not automatically satisfy every food-safety requirement, and meeting food-safety requirements does not mean a product meets every indicator of a quality standard.

For general readers, the scope and classification sections are the most useful. A label that says "Nongxiangxing baijiu" can be read as a statement of a process and flavour type, not a quality grade. Whether a specific bottle follows this standard, and which edition it follows, still depends on the standard code and production information on the label.

Key concepts and classification

The core process features of Nongxiangxing baijiu are commonly summarised as solid-state fermentation in mud pits, continued use of part of the previous fermented grain, and combined steaming of grain and distillation. "Continued use" means that part of the fermented grain from the previous round is retained and enters the next round with fresh material, creating a continuously used grain system. Combined steaming and distillation handles grain steaming and spirit distillation in one flow. Together these practices shape the pit aroma, grain aroma and mellow sweetness of Nongxiangxing baijiu.

For raw materials, Nongxiangxing baijiu often uses sorghum as the main grain, together with wheat, maize, rice and glutinous rice, and uses wheat and other grains to make medium-temperature qu. Multi-grain and single-grain formulas bring different aromatic layers and body, but the formula is not the only criterion of quality; process control, pit condition and storage also matter.

In style terms, Nongxiangxing baijiu is often described as having a pronounced pit aroma, mellow sweetness, harmonious aroma and a clean finish. These are sensory descriptions, not chemical indicators, and they do not constitute a quality ranking. Within the same aroma type, products from different regions, with different grain ratios and different storage periods vary considerably. Sichuan, including Luzhou and Yibin, and Bozhou in Anhui are important producing regions; Wuliangye serves as a concrete example of the multi-grain Nongxiangxing style.

Note that aroma-type classification and product classification are not the same thing. The aroma type describes a combination of aroma and process; product classification may also divide products by alcohol content, total acidity, total esters and other indicators. Classification in the standard serves testing and judgement; consumers should not read a particular class name as "superior" or "purer".

How it relates to other standards

Within the standards system, GB/T 10781.1-2021 has the most direct relationship with two foundational standards. GB/T 15109-2021 Terminology of baijiu industry provides basic terms for the baijiu industry, while GB/T 17204-2021 Terminology and classification of alcoholic beverages provides the classification framework at the level of alcoholic beverages. The former explains what words such as qu, fermentation pit, base liquor and blending mean; the latter explains where baijiu sits among alcoholic beverages. The product standard then sets specific quality requirements for Nongxiangxing baijiu on top of these concepts.

When reading a product standard, it therefore helps to know both its upstream terminology standard and its downstream test-method standards. Inconsistent terminology makes the same sentence point to different objects; unclear test methods make indicators impossible to verify. This site records terminology, classification and product standards separately precisely to avoid mixing requirements from different levels.

Baijiu is the broader style category, and Nongxiangxing is one aroma-type branch. When writing about a region or a specific drink, one can first confirm that it is baijiu, then whether it is Nongxiangxing, and finally which product standard it follows. This order reduces the errors of treating an aroma type as a drink category or a brand as a standard.

What it means for readers

First, it gives the word "Nongxiangxing" on a label a checkable basis. Producers can organise production and testing against the standard, and consumers can use it to judge whether a claimed type has a corresponding standard framework. Whether a label claim is true still needs production records, test reports and regulatory information.

Second, it helps readers understand that a standard code is not decoration. GB/T 10781.1-2021 on a label means the product follows that edition; if it says 2006, the older edition is being followed. Only the code together with the year reveals how old the text is.

Third, it reminds readers to separate "meeting the standard" from "tasting good". The standard sets a basic threshold and rules of expression; flavour preference, price and drinking context are a different set of judgements. Equating "meets the national standard" with "high quality" ignores that a standard is only a minimum consensus.

Points to watch

For GB/T 10781.1-2021 Quality requirements for baijiu — Part 1: Nongxiangxing baijiu, the standard text is protected by copyright. This standard records only public metadata such as the standard code, title, dates and technical committee, and explains the standard's role and structure in original words. It does not reproduce the standard. For specific clauses, indicators or test steps, consult the official full-text system or a formally published copy.

Cite the full code and year. GB/T 10781.1 has 2006 and 2021 editions; omitting the year merges different texts. For translation or cross-language citation, also note the English rendering of the aroma type. The official English title uses Nongxiangxing as a transliteration, which differs from the descriptive phrase strong aroma used in everyday English; formal citation should follow the official English title.

Finally, do not treat a product standard as a brand review. A standard applies to a class of products, not to one brand or one bottle; products at different price points and in different styles can sit under the same standard. Judging a specific drink still requires the label, region, process and actual tasting.

Strong-aroma baijiu and GB/T 15109-2021 Terminology of baijiu industry are the two useful reference points for GB/T 10781.1-2021 Quality requirements for baijiu — Part 1. Strong-aroma baijiu sets out issuer; GB/T 15109-2021 Terminology of baijiu industry brings in scope. Read them together.
