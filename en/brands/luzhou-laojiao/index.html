Luzhou Laojiao

A strong-aroma baijiu brand within the Luzhou Laojiao Group system, with core products including Guojiao 1573 and Luzhou Laojiao Tequ. The name relates to pit age, but the name is not the age of the liquid.

Luzhou Laojiao is a strong-aroma baijiu brand within the Luzhou Laojiao Group Co., Ltd. system. Under the brand there is a high-end line such as Guojiao 1573 and products covering other price bands such as Luzhou Laojiao Tequ. The "old pit" in the name refers to the tradition of continuous mud-pit use, not the age of the liquid in a bottle.

Figure: Luzhou Laojiao. Image: Xiaomage de, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons (source page).

Definition and position

Luzhou Laojiao is first a brand name. It belongs to the strong-aroma route of baijiu and comes from Luzhou, Sichuan. It is not the same concept as "Luzhou Laojiao Group" or "Luzhou Laojiao Co., Ltd.": the group and the company are legal entities, the brand is a name and product system, and the product is a particular bottle.

Mixing registered place, aroma type and brand leads to the wrong conclusion that every strong-aroma spirit from Luzhou is Luzhou Laojiao. When checking information, establish the layer first, then read the label.

Relationship with the producer

The brand is held within the group system, while the listed company handles production and disclosure. The group handles resources and industrial layout, the listed company handles production and sales, the brand handles names and series, and the product handles strength, volume and price. Data scopes differ and must not be mixed.

For Luzhou Laojiao, begin with the product line. Then look at the producer named on the label, and finally ask the market it is sold in. The answers point to different choices, not to a single score.

Brand and product system

Guojiao 1573 is the high-end line, commonly 52% ABV in 500 mL; Luzhou Laojiao Tequ covers the mid-price band; Touqu, Erqu and other series extend further down. The series differ in body design, base-spirit selection and price position while sharing strong-aroma process and brand endorsement.

A series name is not proof of grade, and price is not quality. Check the specific product name, producer, strength and volume on the bottle rather than only the four characters "Luzhou Laojiao" or the number "1573".

Origin and raw materials

Luzhou lies in southeastern Sichuan, where the Yangtze and Tuo rivers meet, with a warm humid climate suited to solid-state mud-pit fermentation. Strong-aroma baijiu depends on continuous pit use, and the microbial community in the pit mud stabilises with age. Within the map of China's baijiu regions, Luzhou is an important strong-aroma node.

The main raw material is sorghum with wheat qu, and some products use multi-grain ratios. Sorghum starch and grain structure affect fermentation start-up, while qu microbial composition affects pit and grain aroma. The ingredient list and standard are what consumers can check directly.

Process and flavour

Strong aroma uses solid-state mud-pit fermentation, continued mash recycling and mixed steaming and boiling, with fractional cutting after removal, graded storage and blending. Qu making and pit maintenance together decide aroma depth and cleanliness, while storage vessel and time affect how quickly harshness fades.

The aroma is led by pit and grain notes, often with sweetness; the entry is mellow and sweet, the body relatively full, and the finish clean. Different pits, ages and blends create clear differences, and one description cannot cover all products.

Identity and labels

For Luzhou Laojiao, the producer, product name, aroma type, strength, volume and standard on the label are the basics for identity. The product standard for strong-aroma baijiu is commonly GB/T 10781.1, but the specific product follows the bottle. Anti-counterfeit marks, batch and channel information also matter.

"1573" is both a cultural marker of pit age and a product name. Treating it as the age of the liquid is the most common misreading. Real age information should follow the label and public company statements, not inference from the name.

History and narrative

For Luzhou Laojiao, public information shows that the pit group begun in 1573 was listed as a Major Historical and Cultural Site Protected at the National Level in 1996; the listed company was listed in 1994, and the group was founded in 2000. These milestones have heritage and public registration support and can be checked.

Earlier distilling tradition mostly belongs to local gazetteers and brand narrative, and specific dates need archival support. Company history, brand age and product age should be read separately rather than compressed into a simple "older is better" logic.

Buying and drinking

The traditional serve is neat in a small cup at room temperature. At the table it suits savoury, sauce-flavoured and mildly spicy Sichuan dishes such as twice-cooked pork, boiled fish, braised dishes and braised snacks; too much heat amplifies the alcohol and too much sweetness covers the pit aroma. Banquets and gifting are common occasions, but moderation still applies.

In mixed drinks, 52% ABV significantly changes the balance and sugar and acid need rebalancing. Ice is not traditional and lowers aroma release; for a lighter impression, a lower-strength version suits better than a lot of ice.

Common misconceptions

First, treating "1573" as the age of the liquid. It is first of all a pit and product name. Second, conflating group, joint-stock company and brand. The three are different layers. Third, treating every strong-aroma spirit from Luzhou as Luzhou Laojiao. Origin is not brand. Fourth, treating series products as "inferior". They are official product lines. Fifth, judging authenticity from packaging or price. Anti-counterfeit checks, channels and labels are more reliable.

Short definition: A strong-aroma baijiu brand within the Luzhou Laojiao Group system, with core products including Guojiao 1573 and Luzhou Laojiao Tequ. The name relates to pit age, but the name is not the age of the liquid.
