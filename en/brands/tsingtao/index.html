Tsingtao Beer is a beer brand originating in Qingdao in 1903 and held by Tsingtao Brewery Company Limited. The brand's main products are lagers, spanning the Classic 1903, pure draft, white beer, Augerta and dark beer series, and it is one of the best-known beer brands in China.

Figure: Tsingtao Beer. Image: Lennartbj, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons (source page). Category photograph of the Qingdao brewery; not an official product pack shot.

Brand definition

A brand and a producer are two different entities. The brand answers "what is this bottle called and whom is it for"; the producer answers "who owns the trademark, who organises production and who bears legal responsibility". This page records the brand layer: name, product line, positioning and marks; see the producer entry for corporate history and listing information.

A brand is also not a single product. One brand has several series, strengths, volumes and channel versions, so a question such as "what does Tsingtao Beer taste like" or "how much does Tsingtao Beer cost" needs a specific product first.

Owning producer

The brand's producer is Tsingtao Brewery Company Limited. Its predecessor, the Germania Brewery Company Qingdao, was founded by German and British merchants in 1903; the joint-stock company was registered in 1993, and its H shares and A shares were listed the same year. Ownership of the brand and trademark, the organisation of production and disclosure rest with that company and its subsidiaries.

The producer entry records the registration date, listing points and business scale. This page keeps only what relates directly to the brand, so corporate history and product history are not conflated.

Product line

Public product material lists the Classic 1903, pure draft, white beer, Augerta and dark beer series. Classic 1903 serves mass retail and restaurant channels, pure draft emphasises filtration and freshness, white beer and dark beer differ more clearly in flavour, and Augerta sits towards the mid-to-high end.

The number in "Classic 1903" points to the brand's origin year, not the age of the beer. Strength, volume, original gravity, ingredients and packaging change by year and channel, and some products have regional or festive versions.

Buyers should check the product name, strength, original gravity, ingredients, producer and production date on the bottle or can rather than judging by colour or series name alone.

Positioning and price band

The brand covers a band from mass market to mid-to-high end: the Classic series serves everyday drinking and restaurants, pure draft and white beer target consumers who care about freshness and flavour, and series such as Augerta reach into mid-to-high-end gifting and gatherings.

Prices change with year, channel, promotions and pack size, so this page gives no specific retail price and does not treat one promotion as long-term positioning. A price band is market positioning, not a quality grade; judging the liquid means looking at raw materials, process, freshness and label information.

History

In 1903 German and British merchants founded the Germania Brewery Company Qingdao, bringing German brewing equipment and techniques; the brand narrative of Tsingtao Beer starts there. In 1993 the joint-stock company was registered, and its H shares and A shares were listed in Hong Kong and Shanghai, taking the brand into the capital market.

In 2008 and 2022 the company became an official sponsor of the Beijing Olympics and the Beijing Winter Olympics, extending brand exposure to venues and sports events. On the company profile's figures, 2025 full-year product sales reached 7.648 million kilolitres and revenue was RMB 32.47 billion.

In the brand history, "founded in 1903" and "listed in 1993" are two different points and cannot be merged into one year, and event sponsorship is not a product quality certification.

Name and marks

Qingdao is a place name and beer is a category, so the Chinese brand name is simply the place plus the category. The English trademark is spelled TSINGTAO, a postal-style romanisation kept from history and different from the modern pinyin QINGDAO.

Trademarks, bottle shapes, colours and label layout all belong to the brand's mark system, but counterfeit products can imitate the look. Buyers should check the producer, anti-counterfeiting marks, batch and official channel rather than bottle shape or dominant colour alone.

The place name in the brand refers to the historical origin and does not mean every bottle is made in Qingdao today. The company has plants in many parts of China, and the specific origin should be taken from the label.

Common misconceptions

First, conflating the brand with the joint-stock company. The brand is a market mark; the company is a legal entity. Second, treating 1903 as the year the joint-stock company was founded. 1903 is the founding of the predecessor brewery. Third, treating "Classic 1903" as the age of the beer. The number points to the brand's origin year. Fourth, treating "pure draft" as higher strength or a higher grade. Pure draft describes filtration and heat treatment. Fifth, treating the Qingdao origin as the filling location of every product. Use the producer and origin on the label.

Tsingtao Brewery Company Limited and Beer are the nearest useful comparisons: one clarifies owning company, the other tests product lines.

Short definition: A beer brand originating in Qingdao in 1903 and held by Tsingtao Brewery Company Limited. Its main products are lagers, spanning the Classic 1903, pure draft, white beer and Augerta series.

To read Tsingtao Beer, start with the product line, then the producer named on the label, then the market it is sold in; no single check settles it.

Tsingtao is China's oldest beer brand, founded in Qingdao in 1903; it is a pale lager with about 4% ABV, and the brand also owns other breweries and exports widely. The Tsingtao Beer brand should be distinguished from the city and the company; different products carry different strengths and packaging.

The brand's green bottle and red label are widely recognised, but the label's producer and ABV still decide what is inside.
