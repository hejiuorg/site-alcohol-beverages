Red Star

A light-aroma baijiu brand under Beijing Red Star Co., Ltd., built around the er-guotou cut-the-head-and-tail craft; the blue-bottle mellow series and green-bottle traditional series form the everyday core, with Red Star Gaozhao for gifting and commemoration.

Red Star Erguotou is the first Beijing baijiu many visitors meet: a clear glass bottle, a simple red label and a clean, direct palate. The brand belongs to Beijing Red Star Co., Ltd. and is built on the er-guotou practice of cutting the head and tail and keeping the middle, within the light-aroma route. What makes Red Star distinctive is not expense but the way it ties a craft tradition, a city memory and a mass price band together. Understanding it means looking at the craft, the product line and the relationship between the brand and the category name "er-guotou".

1949: Beijing's first state distillery

In May 1949 the Huabei Liquor Monopoly Experimental Factory was founded. It was the first state-run distillery in Beijing and the predecessor of Red Star. After founding, it absorbed twelve old distilleries including Yuanshenghao, concentrating scattered workshop skills in a state production system. The first Red Star Erguotou entered the market as a gift for the founding of the People's Republic, an episode that became the brand's most important historical narrative. The distinction to keep is that 1949 is the start of the state factory, not the start of er-guotou craft.

1680: Yuanshenghao and the cut

The brand traces its craft to 1680. The three Zhao brothers, Zhao Cunren, Zhao Cunyi and Zhao Cunli, founded Yuanshenghao near Qianmen and improved the distillation method: the first "head" and the last "tail" are separated, and only the middle cut is kept. The practice gives a cleaner spirit with fewer off-notes and is one reason for the name er-guotou, literally "second pot". In 2008 the traditional brewing craft of Beijing er-guotou liquor entered the national intangible cultural heritage list.

Product lines: blue, green and Gaozhao

The blue bottle is the mellow series launched by Red Star in 2009. It is commonly 43% vol and 53% vol, with 150 mL, 250 mL, 500 mL and 750 mL formats, declares sorghum and water as ingredients, and belongs to the light-aroma type. The green bottle is the more traditional form, commonly 56% vol in 500 mL, with a more direct flavour. Red Star Gaozhao sits higher, with Zongshi 1949 at 52% vol in 500 mL for gifting and commemoration. The three lines differ clearly in style and price and cannot be summed up by the single word "Red Star".

Price bands and occasions

The green bottle commonly sells at 10-20 yuan and is a typical open-bottle everyday spirit; the blue bottle sits at roughly 20-30 yuan and stresses softness and drinkability; Red Star Gaozhao can reach 100-300 yuan for gifting and banquets. Red Star's core competitiveness is availability and consistent style rather than scarcity. A price band is positioning, not a quality grade; equating a low price with low quality, or sales volume with quality, are common misreadings.

Packaging and recognition

Red Star's visual identity has long stayed simple: a clear bottle, red-and-white or blue labels, and prominent Red Star characters. The blue label marks the mellow series, the green label the traditional series, and Gaozhao uses a heavier bottle and gift box. Simple packaging is easier to imitate, so buyers should check the full producer name, anti-counterfeiting mark, batch and execution standard rather than label colour alone.

Channels and market

Red Star is strongest in Beijing, covering restaurants, supermarkets, convenience stores and e-commerce, with high open-bottle distribution in catering. Outside Beijing it relies on the regional association of "Beijing er-guotou" and on value for money. Red Star and Niulanshan both belong to the Beijing er-guotou camp and overlap heavily in the open-bottle price band, so competition is long-running. For consumers that means more choice, and more reason to check product name and specification.

Historical points

Yuanshenghao was founded in 1680. In May 1949 the state experimental factory was established and absorbed twelve old distilleries. In 2008 the traditional er-guotou craft entered the national intangible heritage list. In 2009 the blue-bottle mellow series was launched. In September 2025 the eighth generation of Red Star Erguotou completed its changeover, with the blue bottle upgraded alongside it. Read together, these points show four layers: workshop, state factory, heritage designation and product iteration, not a single line.

Honours and controversy

The company's public identity includes China Time-honoured Brand and status as a protection unit for a national intangible cultural heritage representative project; Red Star Gaozhao Zongshi 1949 has also appeared in a Beijing low-carbon consumer product list. Controversy centres on three issues. First, er-guotou is a category name, not a brand exclusive to Red Star, and other companies may make it. Second, open-bottle spirits are often judged by price rather than by craft and execution standard. Third, Red Star and Niulanshan products are close in name, bottle shape and price, which makes them easy to confuse at the shelf.

Reading a bottle

Begin with the product name: blue bottle, green bottle, Gaozhao or a commemorative edition. Then check strength and volume, since 43% vol, 53% vol and 56% vol taste very different. Next read ingredients and the execution standard to confirm a light-aroma baijiu. Finally check producer and batch. A bottle that says only "er-guotou" without a brand and producer is giving incomplete information.

Common misconceptions

First, er-guotou is a craft and category name, not Red Star's brand name. Second, 1680 is not the founding year of the Red Star company. Third, 1949 is not the starting point of er-guotou craft. Fourth, a cheap open-bottle spirit is not automatically low quality. Fifth, blue bottle and green bottle are not the same liquid in different colours.

Short definition: A light-aroma baijiu brand under Beijing Red Star Co., Ltd., built around the er-guotou cut-the-head-and-tail craft; the blue-bottle mellow series and green-bottle traditional series form the everyday core, with Red Star Gaozhao for gifting and commemoration.

Start with the product line. From there, consider the producer named on the label, then the market it is sold in. That is how a practical judgment about Red Star is built.
