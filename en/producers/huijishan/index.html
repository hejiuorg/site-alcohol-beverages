Kuaijishan Shaoxing Rice Wine Co., Ltd. is a huangjiu producer in Shaoxing, Zhejiang, and its products belong to the Shaoxing type of huangjiu. The company trades under the Kuaijishan brand, with a representative product in Kuaijishan Chunzheng 5. This producer records the producer layer: company identity, site, brand structure and craft tradition.

Company identity

This producer records the producer layer: company name, location, historical starting point and organisational form. The company is the production and operating entity, holding the trademark and running production and sales. When discussing "Kuaijishan", first separate the company, the brand and a single bottle.

Kuaijishan is a listed public company, listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange in 2014. Public material places the historical starting point in 1743, with the Yunji winery in Shaoxing as the predecessor. That year belongs to the company narrative as a workshop origin and is not the founding date of the modern joint-stock company.

Production area and site

Shaoxing lies in northern Zhejiang south of the Qiantang River, with a subtropical monsoon climate: humid, rainy and with four distinct seasons. The Jian Lake water system is an important water source for Shaoxing wine. Traditional practice values winter brewing, using low winter temperatures to slow fermentation and limit unwanted microbes, and the habit continues today.

The plant is in Shaoxing, and the exact address and visiting arrangements follow the company's current notice. Regional conditions are more than a narrative: water quality, temperature, airborne microbes and glutinous-rice supply all land in concrete fermentation outcomes. The geographical indication fixes origin and process requirements, but producers and batches within the same region still differ.

Brands and products

The main brand is Kuaijishan, with products across age statements, volumes and price bands. Five-year aged wine is one of the more widely distributed tiers, represented by Kuaijishan Chunzheng 5, and the company also offers premium series and gift products.

Age statements, sugar types and packaging can differ between product lines, and one product cannot describe the whole company. Buyers should check producer, strength, net volume, sugar level and standard on the bottle label. Age statements must be read together with the company's product explanation, not taken from a number on the package alone.

Brewing tradition

The typical Shaoxing route is: steep rice, steam, cool, mix with qu, ferment in vats, stir (kaipa), press, pasteurise, fill jars and age. Glutinous rice supplies starch, wheat qu handles saccharification and part of the aroma formation, and wine starter is used to start fermentation. Stirring regulates temperature and oxygen and is the key step in steering fermentation.

After fermentation the liquid is pressed off, pasteurised and stored in jars. Ceramic jars are slightly permeable, and the wine slowly oxidises and esterifies during storage to form aged aroma. Company material emphasises hand-made winter brewing and long storage in jars; these statements are useful clues to the craft, but exact parameters vary by product line.

Company history

Public material traces Kuaijishan to the Yunji winery of 1743. Public material states that in 1915 Shaoxing wine won an award at the Panama-Pacific International Exposition, an experience the company repeatedly cites as part of its brand history. The company then continued production in Shaoxing and gradually built a product structure centred on aged wine.

In 2014 the company listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange and entered the capital markets. The listing brought capacity expansion and brand investment, but the main business remained Shaoxing huangjiu. Overlaying the three identities of long-established brand, geographical-indication product and listed company makes the company's position easier to understand.

Timeline

  • 1915: public material states that Shaoxing wine won an award at the Panama-Pacific International Exposition.
  • 2014: Kuaijishan listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange.

Industry and market

The Shaoxing huangjiu industry is characterised by many small and medium producers and strong seasonality, and the pace of production during winter brewing differs from the rest of the year. Kuaijishan sits within this cluster and must maintain traditional craft while facing structural issues such as regionally concentrated consumption and limited cultivation of younger drinkers.

Huangjiu spans a wide price range, from everyday cooking and table wine to gift and aged products. Price discussions should state whether the figure is the official guide price, supermarket retail or restaurant pricing, and no single number describes the national market. This producer gives no specific prices.

Common misconceptions

First, treating 1743 as the founding year of the modern company. It is the workshop origin in the company narrative. Second, treating "Kuaijishan" as only one bottle. It is at once a company name, a brand name and a product-series name. Third, treating an age number as the legal age of the liquid. Age statements should be read with the company's product explanation. Fourth, treating the geographical indication as a quality grade. It fixes origin and process requirements. Fifth, judging authenticity by price or packaging. Channels, anti-counterfeit marks and labels are more reliable.
