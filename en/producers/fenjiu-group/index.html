Shanxi Xinghuacun Fenjiu Group Co., Ltd. is based in Xinghuacun, Fenyang, Shanxi, and is a wholly state-owned company. Public information shows that it was restructured in March 2002 from the former Shanxi Xinghuacun Fenjiu (Group) Company. Its listed arm is Shanxi Xinghuacun Fenjiu Distillery Co., Ltd., stock code 600809. Group, listed company, brand and specific product are four layers that must be checked separately.

Company identity

This producer records producer identity: name, registered place, founding time, organisational form and public entry point. The group handles state-owned asset operation and industrial management, while the joint-stock company handles production, sales and disclosure. The names are similar, but the legal entities and financial scope differ, so group statements cannot be applied directly to the listed company's reports.

In public material, "Fenjiu Group" usually means the parent group described here, while "Shanxi Xinghuacun Fenjiu Distillery Co., Ltd." is the listed entity. Figures on output, revenue and channels should follow the joint-stock company's periodic reports.

Relationship with the listed company

Official information shows that Shanxi Xinghuacun Fenjiu Distillery Co., Ltd. was restructured in December 1993 and listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange in January 1994. The company describes itself publicly as "China's first baijiu stock"; that is a company statement and can be checked against exchange records.

The joint-stock company's main business is Fenjiu, Zhuyeqing and series products. The group also has industrial investment and support businesses whose structure changes by year. The two sets of figures must not be mixed, especially when comparing years and growth rates.

Origin and environment

Fenyang lies in central Shanxi with a temperate continental monsoon climate: distinct seasons and limited rainfall. The groundwater and loess environment around Xinghuacun suit separated jar fermentation. Within the wider map of China's baijiu regions, Fenyang is an important light-aroma node, contrasting with Sichuan strong aroma and Guizhou sauce aroma.

The "light" in light aroma is both a process requirement and a flavour direction. Jar fermentation separates the mash from soil and reduces the influence of pit microbes, so the aroma is cleaner and fresher. Regional tradition and archaeological finds are often mentioned, but specific dates and interpretations of objects need archaeological reports; this producer does not turn legend into dated fact.

Raw materials and process position

Fenjiu uses sorghum as the raw material and barley and peas for qu making. "Qingzheng erciqing" means the raw material and mash are steamed separately, with two fermentations and two distillations, emphasising the word "clean": clean steaming, clean distillation, jars and clean fermentation. This separation reduces sources of off-flavour and explains why light aroma is assessed differently from strong aroma.

The qu raw materials and culture temperature shape the aroma direction, while jar cleanliness and temperature control affect fermentation stability. These are company processes with limited public detail; what consumers can check directly are the ingredient list, standard and strength on the label.

Products and brand layers

Besides Fenjiu, the group also has liqueur products such as Zhuyeqing. Under the Fenjiu brand there are series such as Qinghua, Laobaifen and Bofen, and products split further by strength, volume and packaging. The brand answers what it is called, the company answers who makes it, and the product answers what is in the bottle.

Baijiu is the broad category to which Fenjiu belongs, with light aroma as its main style. Mixing group history, brand age and product age is one of the most common errors in reading baijiu information.

History

The joint-stock company was restructured in December 1993 and listed in January 1994; the group was restructured in March 2002. Earlier workshop history mostly belongs to local gazetteers and corporate narrative, and specific years need archival support. This producer keeps only checkable corporate milestones and does not assemble contradictory details from different sources into one "complete timeline".

Limits of public information

This producer does not expand revenue, capacity, share price or market share. These need full context and change over time; taken out of the reporting year they mislead. Titles such as "time-honoured brand" or "famous liquor" should be checked against the issuing body and year rather than judged from packaging.

Common misconceptions

First, conflating the group with the joint-stock company. Their legal identities and disclosure duties differ. Second, treating "China's first baijiu stock" as a third-party conclusion. It is a company statement. Third, equating archaeological finds with the origin of modern process. Each needs separate argument. Fourth, assuming light aroma means "no aroma". Light aroma stresses cleanliness and freshness, not absence. Fifth, judging quality from packaging or price. Labels, standards and channels are more reliable.

Fenjiu Group is a state-owned company, while Shanxi Xinghuacun Fenjiu Factory is the listed platform. Group, listed company, brand and product line are four different layers. The group’s history cannot simply be transferred to the listed company, and the Fenjiu trademark is not the same as one specific bottle. Read the equity structure and the business scope before drawing conclusions about a product.

Start with the range it makes. From there, consider the site and the owner, then the bottler named on the label. That is how a practical judgment about Shanxi Xinghuacun Fenjiu Group Co., Ltd. is built.
