Yantai Changyu Pioneer Wine Co., Ltd. is a wine producer based in Yantai, Shandong. In 1892 Zhang Bishi founded the Changyu winemaking company in Yantai, opening the history of industrial wine production in China. In 1997 the company was established as a joint-stock company by public offer, and its B shares were listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange the same year; its A shares were listed in 2000. Its main products are wine, covered in the Changyu entry.

Company identity

This producer records the producer layer: company name, registered place, founding date and organisational form. The joint-stock company is the listed entity and handles production, sales and disclosure; the group layer involves investment and winery assets. When discussing "Changyu", first establish whether the subject is the joint-stock company, the group, the brand or a bottle.

Public material uses different starting points for "founding year": counting from the joint-stock company gives 1997, while counting from the Changyu winemaking company goes back to 1892. This producer uses 1997 for the founding year and lists the 1892 founding separately in the history section.

Site and region

Yantai lies in the north-east of the Shandong Peninsula. Maritime influence moderates the climate and lengthens the growing season, and the area is one of the birthplaces of China's modern wine industry. Grape varieties, soils and the maritime climate together affect ripeness and acidity, and therefore the style of the wine. Within the map of China's wine regions, Yantai, Ningxia, Xinjiang and Hebei each have different climate conditions and variety structures.

A site in Yantai does not mean every product uses grapes from the same region. Different wineries, product lines and sourcing arrangements should be checked against the producer, region and standard on the label.

Brands and products

Changyu is the core brand, and its products cover red wine, white wine, sparkling wine and sweet wines. In public product material, the Cabernet series is a widely distributed core item, while winery wines are distinguished by region, variety and vintage. Product lines, vintages, volumes and prices change by year, so buyers should use the current label and official channels.

"Cabernet" here is a brand and product-series name, not a grape-variety name. The variety, region and vintage on the label are the main basis for judging where the wine comes from.

Craft and style

The basic wine route is: harvest, sorting, crushing, fermentation, pressing, clarification, ageing and blending. Red wine ferments with the skins to extract colour and tannin, while white wine usually ferments without skins; some products are aged in oak to add aroma and structure. Yeast strain, fermentation temperature and ageing time all affect the final style.

A process description states the style route only and cannot replace a judgement about a specific product. Products at different prices and from different regions within the same company may differ in raw-material grade, ageing method and blending ratio.

History

In 1892 Zhang Bishi invested in the Changyu winemaking company, introduced grape varieties, built cellars and began industrial production in Yantai. In 1997 Yantai Changyu Group Co., Ltd. acted as sole promoter and established Yantai Changyu Pioneer Wine Co., Ltd. by public offer; its B shares were listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange the same year. In 2000 its A shares were listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange. The company later expanded its product lines through winery construction and overseas investment.

"Founding", "establishing the joint-stock company" and "listing" are different points and cannot be merged into one founding year. The brand history goes back to 1892, but the governance and disclosure history of the joint-stock company starts in 1997.

Timeline

  • 1997: the joint-stock company is established by public offer and its B shares are listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange.
  • 2000: its A shares are listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange.

Visiting

The company is in Zhifu District, Yantai. Opening hours, booking arrangements and ticket prices for drinks-culture facilities such as the Changyu Wine Culture Museum depend on the operator's current notices. This producer does not provide unverified booking numbers or tour prices and does not recommend following old guides to the site.

Common misconceptions

First, treating 1997 as the start of the Changyu brand's history. Joint-stock establishment and the 1892 founding are different points. Second, treating "Cabernet" as a grape variety. It is a brand and product-series name. Third, treating the Yantai site as the grape origin of every product. Use the region on the label. Fourth, treating "winery wine" as a fixed grade. The winery, region and vintage all need to be checked. Fifth, treating a vintage as a guarantee of quality. A vintage describes climatic conditions, not a style ranking.

Changyu is a wine company, not a single brand. It was founded by Zhang Bishi, later became a joint-stock company and is now listed; its labels cover estates, brands and products at different price levels. Historic claims about the company’s founding belong to corporate history, not to the age of a particular bottle. Read the producer, the brand and the appellation separately before judging a wine.

To read Yantai Changyu Pioneer Wine Co., Ltd., start with the range it makes, then the site and the owner, then the bottler named on the label; no single check settles it.
