Zhejiang Taipai Shaoxing Rice Wine Co., Ltd. is a huangjiu producer in Shaoxing, and its products belong to the Shaoxing type of huangjiu. The company trades under the Taipai brand, with a representative product in Taipai Handmade Winter Brew. Production area and site

Shaoxing lies in northern Zhejiang south of the Qiantang River, with a humid and rainy subtropical monsoon climate and four distinct seasons. The Jian Lake water system is an important water source for Shaoxing wine. Traditional Shaoxing practice values winter brewing, using low winter temperatures to slow fermentation and limit unwanted microbes.

The plant is in Shaoxing, and the exact address and visiting arrangements follow the company's current notice. The geographical indication fixes origin, raw-material and process requirements, but producers and batches within the same region still differ, and label information remains the basis for checking a product.

Brands and products

The main brand is Taipai, with Shaoxing wine at the core and a range covering handmade winter brewing, aged wine and different packaging. Taipai Handmade Winter Brew is one of the more recognisable products.

Age statements, sugar types, volumes and standards can differ between product lines, and one product cannot describe the whole company. Buyers should check producer, strength, net volume, sugar level and standard on the bottle label.

Handmade winter brewing

"Handmade winter brewing" is a product and craft statement: fermentation starts in vats at low winter temperatures, stirring is done by hand to regulate temperature and oxygen, and the wine is then pressed, pasteurised, filled into jars and aged. Slow fermentation at low temperature means a long cycle and high demands on temperature control and operator experience.

Glutinous rice supplies starch, wheat qu handles saccharification and part of the aroma formation, and wine starter is used to start fermentation. After fermentation the liquid is pressed off, pasteurised and stored in ceramic jars. Ceramic jars are slightly permeable, and the wine slowly oxidises and esterifies to form aged aroma.

"Handmade" describes a mode of operation, not a quality grade. Handmade winter brewing and mechanised production can coexist, and quality still depends on raw materials, process control and storage conditions.

Traditionally, Shaoxing wine is divided by recipe and sugar level into yuanhong, jiafan, huadiao, shanniang and xiangxue types, which differ in the water added, the proportion of extra rice and whether wine replaces water. Jiafan adds glutinous rice for a fuller body; huadiao usually means aged jiafan sold in jars; shanniang uses wine instead of water and is sweeter; xiangxue uses distilled spirit or strong liquor instead of water and is very sweet. GB/T 17946-2008 sets the geographical-indication area, raw materials and process for Shaoxing wine, with Jian Lake water, glutinous rice, wheat qu and wine starter as core elements. The classification and age statements of Taipai Handmade Winter Brew still follow its bottle label and declared standard.

Export and market

Public material presents export as an important market for Taipai. Shaoxing wine has steady demand in overseas Chinese markets and Asian restaurant channels, and export products usually need to meet destination-country rules on labelling, additives and strength declaration, so the same wine may carry different labels in different markets.

Export volumes, market rankings and prices change over time, and this producer gives no specific figures. When discussing market position, company statements, industry statistics and third-party reports should be distinguished.

Industry position

The Shaoxing huangjiu industry is characterised by many small and medium producers and strong seasonality, and the pace of production during winter brewing differs from the rest of the year. Taipai sits within this cluster and must maintain traditional craft while facing structural issues such as regionally concentrated consumption and limited cultivation of younger drinkers.

Huangjiu spans a wide price range, from everyday products to gift and aged products. Prices vary by channel, region and supply, and this producer gives no specific figures.

Common misconceptions

First, treating "handmade winter brewing" as a quality grade. It describes a mode of operation and a seasonal schedule. Second, treating Taipai as an export-only brand. It also sells products in the domestic market. Third, treating an age number as the age of the liquid. Age statements should be read with the company's product explanation. Fourth, treating the geographical indication as a quality certificate. It fixes origin and process requirements. Fifth, judging authenticity by price or packaging. Channels, anti-counterfeit marks and labels are more reliable.
