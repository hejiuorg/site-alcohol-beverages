Jiangsu Yanghe Group Co., Ltd. is based in Suqian, Jiangsu, and is a local state-owned enterprise group in the baijiu industry. Public registration information shows a founding date in May 1997. Its listed arm is Jiangsu Yanghe Distillery Co., Ltd., stock code 002304, listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange in 2009. Group, listed company, brand and specific product are different layers.

Company identity

This producer records producer identity: name, registered place, founding time, organisational form and public entry point. The group leans towards asset operation and industrial management, while the joint-stock company handles production, sales and disclosure. The names are similar, but the legal entities and financial scope differ and must not be mixed.

In public material, "Yanghe Group" usually means the parent group described here; "Jiangsu Yanghe Distillery Co., Ltd." is the listed entity, and the official site also uses "Sujiu Group" as a brand-style name. When checking information, first establish whether the subject is the group, the joint-stock company or a product series.

Relationship with the listed company

The official site and exchange filings show that Jiangsu Yanghe Distillery Co., Ltd. was established in December 2002 with Yanghe Group as the main promoter, and listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange in November 2009. Its main business is baijiu production and sales, with periodic disclosure under regulatory requirements.

Revenue, profit, capacity and share price therefore belong to the listed company's scope and cannot be treated directly as group-wide figures. The group also has industrial investment and support businesses whose structure changes by year. When comparing years, also note changes in scope and consolidation.

Origin and environment

Suqian lies in northern Jiangsu in the Hongze Lake basin, with a warm-temperate monsoon climate, distinct seasons and moderate rainfall. The area is rich in waterways, and water and soil conditions suit distilling. Within the wider map of China's baijiu regions, Suqian is an important node of Jianghuai strong aroma, differing from Sichuan strong aroma in climate and process emphasis.

Yanghe and Shuanggou are the two traditional production sites in Suqian, and company material lists several production bases in the system. Regional advantage is not one factor but water, grain, pits, qu making and long practice combined. "Wetland region" is a geographical description, not a quality grade.

Raw materials and process position

Yanghe uses sorghum and other grains, with solid-state mud-pit fermentation and continued mash recycling; the main line is strong-aroma baijiu. Company material stresses a "mellow-soft" style, usually linked to low-temperature slow fermentation, longer fermentation cycles, careful cutting and blending; specific parameters are company process with limited public detail.

What consumers can check directly are the ingredient list, standard, strength and volume on the label. Style description is a sensory direction and should not be reduced to "high or low strength" or "high or low price". Mellow-soft does not mean low strength or thin flavour; judgement still comes back to actual tasting.

Products and brand layers

Under the Yanghe brand there are series such as Yanghe Daqu and Blue Classic, and Blue Classic splits into Hai Zhi Lan, Tian Zhi Lan and Meng Zhi Lan; products then split by strength, volume and packaging. The system also includes brands such as Shuanggou. The brand answers what it is called, the company answers who makes it, and the product answers what is in the bottle.

History

Checkable modern milestones are: consolidation of the state-run distillery system in the later 20th century, establishment of the joint-stock company in December 2002, and listing in November 2009. Company material traces the distilling tradition further back, but earlier dates mostly belong to local gazetteers and brand narrative and need archival support; this producer does not force details from different sources into one story.

Limits of public information

This producer does not expand revenue, profit, capacity or market share. These need full context and change over time; taken out of the reporting year they mislead. Titles such as "China Time-honoured Brand" or "famous liquor" should be checked against the issuing body and year, not judged from packaging.

Common misconceptions

First, conflating the group with the joint-stock company. Their legal identities and disclosure duties differ. Second, treating "mellow-soft" as low strength or thin. It is a style direction, not an alcohol statement. Third, treating the registered place as the origin of every product. Use the production address on the label. Fourth, treating a series name as a vintage. A series name is not the age of the liquid. Fifth, judging quality from packaging or price. Labels, standards and channels are more reliable.

Yanghe Group is a baijiu group whose listed platform is Yanghe Distillery. Group, listed company, brand and product line are separate layers. Yanghe built its market identity around a soft, mellow strong-aroma style, but “mellow” is a sensory description, not a grade. Read the equity structure, the business segments and the product structure before making claims about a specific bottle.

Read Jiangsu Yanghe Group Co., Ltd. through the range it makes, then the site and the owner, and finally the bottler named on the label. The sequence matters.
