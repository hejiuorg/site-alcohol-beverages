A multinational spirits company formed when Japan's Suntory Holdings bought the American Beam Inc in 2014; it places American bourbon, Canadian whisky, Scotch and Japanese whisky under one group, runs its operating base from Chicago, and takes its Chinese name from the two existing names Beam and Suntory.

Guizhou Dongjiu Co., Ltd.

A baijiu producer in Donggongsi, Zunyi, Guizhou. Dongjiu is a dong-aroma (also called medicinal-aroma) baijiu; production resumed in 1957, it joined China's old eight famous liquors in 1963, and a 2008 Guizhou local standard named it the representative of dong-aroma.
