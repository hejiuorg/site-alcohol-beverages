Shaanxi Xifeng Liquor Group Co., Ltd. is based in Baoji, Shaanxi. Its predecessor was the state-run Shaanxi Xifeng Distillery, founded in 1956. The company became a joint-stock company in 1999 and was renamed a group company in 2008. Its core product Xifengjiu is a feng-aroma baijiu, covered in the Xifengjiu and Hong Xifeng entries.

Company identity

This producer records the producer layer: company name, registered place, founding date and organisational form. The joint-stock company handles production, sales and disclosure, while the group layer involves investment and supporting industries. When discussing "Xifeng", first establish whether the subject is the joint-stock company, the group, the brand or a bottle.

Public material uses different starting points for "founding year": counting from the restructured joint-stock company gives 1999, while counting from the predecessor state-run distillery goes back to 1956. This producer uses 1999 for the founding year and lists the predecessor separately in the history section, so the two conventions are not mixed.

Site and region

Xifengjiu comes from the Fengxiang area of western Guanzhong, Shaanxi, now part of Fengxiang District in Baoji. The climate is a warm-temperate continental monsoon type, with distinct seasons, concentrated rainfall and relatively dry air, which suits qu making and storage. Local sorghum, water and pit conditions together form the base of the feng-aroma style. Within the map of China's baijiu regions, Fengxiang is one of the most representative famous-liquor origins in the north-west.

The registered address, production site and filling location are not necessarily the same. Product lines, production bases and contract arrangements should be checked against the production address and the standard on the label. Regional conditions are only external factors in style formation; they cannot replace checks on a specific product.

Brands and products

Xifengjiu is the core brand and belongs to the feng-aroma type. Public product material lists series such as Hong Xifeng and Xifeng 1964, with different positions and body designs; the usual strength is 55% vol. Product lines, volumes, strengths, standards and prices change by year, so buyers should rely on the current label and official channels.

Statements such as "vintage" and "wine-sea storage" should be read with the label explanation. A wine sea is a storage vessel, not the exact age of the liquid in the bottle; the age, base-liquor composition and blending ratio should be checked against company information. Similar names and counterfeit products exist in the market, so checking the producer, aroma type, strength and anti-counterfeit information matters more than packaging.

Craft and style

Feng-aroma uses sorghum as the raw material, with barley, wheat and peas for qu making. The mash undergoes solid-state fermentation in earthen cellars, is distilled in fractions through distillation, then stored in wine seas and blended into the finished product. The temperature and ratio in qu making affect saccharification and aroma precursors, while the earthen cellar environment creates a microbial structure different from mud pits.

A wine sea is a barrel-shaped container woven from willow-like strips and lined with traditional materials, in which the liquor changes slowly. Its breathability and lining take part in oxidation and aroma integration and form part of the feng-aroma style. Feng-aroma is often described as lying between light-aroma and strong-aroma, but it is a type formed by its own process, not a simple blend of the other two.

History

In 1956 the state-run Shaanxi Xifeng Distillery was founded, moving Xifengjiu from workshop production into factory production. In July 1996 the Shaanxi provincial government listed the distillery in the second batch of pilot enterprises for a modern enterprise system. In October 1999 the Shaanxi Xifeng Liquor Co., Ltd. was restructured on the basis of the distillery. In October 2008 the company was renamed Shaanxi Xifeng Liquor Group Co., Ltd.; it increased capital twice in 2009 and 2010. In 2010 the Xifeng Liquor Distillery Group Co., Ltd. was converted into an investment-holding group.

"Founding", "restructuring" and "renaming" are three different points in the company's history and cannot be merged into one founding year. The brand history can be traced further back to local distilling traditions, but that is cultural narrative, not the continuous production history of the modern company.

Visiting

The company is in Fengxiang District, Baoji. Opening arrangements for plant tours, drinks-culture displays and tasting events depend on the operator's current official notices. This producer does not provide unverified booking numbers, ticket prices or travel guides and does not recommend following old guides to the site. To learn about the process, prefer officially open industrial-tourism or drinks-culture programmes.

Common misconceptions

First, treating 1999 as the start of all Xifeng history. The joint-stock restructuring and the state-run distillery's founding are different points. Second, treating feng-aroma as a simple compromise between light and strong aroma. It is a type formed by its own process. Third, treating the wine sea as ordinary packaging. It takes part in storage and flavour formation. Fourth, treating the registered place as the origin of every product. Use the production address on the label. Fifth, using "vintage" or "wine-sea" wording in place of a specific age. Vessel and age are different things.

For Shaanxi Xifeng Liquor Group Co., Ltd., begin with the range it makes. Then look at the site and the owner, and finally ask the bottler named on the label. The answers point to different choices, not to a single score.
