Shede Zhihui

A high-end strong-aroma product from Shede, launched in 2018 and commonly bottled at 52%, 42% or 38% ABV in 500 mL. Company material says it uses base spirit stored for more than eight years, seasoned with older spirit.

"Zhihui Shede" is a higher-positioned strong-aroma product under the Shede brand, launched in 2018 and commonly bottled at 52%, 42% or 38% ABV in 500 mL, made in Shehong, Sichuan. It places "old liquor" and "culture" in the same product story, and understanding it means separating the brand, the producer, the base-spirit sourcing and the exact specification.

Definition and position

Zhihui Shede is a core strategic product of the Shede brand, positioned above the more widely distributed Pinwei Shede and aimed at business banquets and gifting. It is still strong-aroma baijiu, not a separate aroma type, and not a statutory "vintage liquor" grade.

The word "wisdom" in the product name comes from the brand's cultural language and is not a quality grade. "Shede" is both a brand name and a product name, so the series name is needed in conversation to point to a specific bottle.

Producer and brand

The product is made by Shede Liquor Co., Ltd. and the brand is carried by Shede. The company is based in Tuopai town, Shehong, Sichuan; its predecessor can be traced to the 1940 Tai'an workshop, a state-run Tuopai Qujiu distillery was set up in 1952, the joint-stock company was registered in 1993, and it listed in 1996.

In December 2001 the company launched the Shede brand on the basis of Tuopai Qujiu, aimed at the high-end market. In January 2018 the company changed its name to Shede Liquor Co., Ltd. Brand and listed company are two layers: the former is a market mark, the latter a legal entity.

Origin and raw materials

Shehong lies in the central Sichuan basin, in the Fu River catchment, with a mild and humid climate suited to open qu making and pit fermentation. Within the map of Sichuan's strong-aroma regions, Shehong is one of the older strong-aroma bases, forming a different style band from Yibin, Luzhou and Mianzhu.

Public product material describes Shede as a multi-grain strong aroma. Sorghum provides the frame and structure, wheat participates in qu making and brings qu aroma and fermentation power, and water affects the cleanliness of the entry and the harmony of the body. The exact ingredient list and ratio are governed by the bottle label.

For Shede Zhihui, a multi-grain recipe is a style route, not a league table in which more grains means better quality. Treating pure grain as the only standard ignores the effect of qu, pits, fermentation length and storage on the body.

Process

The basic strong-aroma path is mud-pit solid-state fermentation, continued mash, mixed steaming and cutting the distillate by stage. Qu making determines the microbial community and aroma precursors, fermentation completes saccharification and alcohol production in the pit, and distillation cuts the run by stage, with the middle section used as the main base spirit.

After long storage, the base spirits are blended to unify aroma, body and finish. Company material says Zhihui Shede uses base spirit stored for more than eight years and seasons it with spirit older than fifteen years. That statement describes the resources behind the product design; it does not mean every drop in the bottle reaches the same age.

In 2019 the company set out an old-liquor strategy, making longer-stored base and seasoning spirits the core of brand differentiation. A strategy is a statement of direction; exact ages, ratios and batches still depend on company disclosures and the label.

Specification and label

Public product material gives the common specifications as 52%, 42% and 38% ABV in 500 mL, with the aroma type marked as strong aroma. Baijiu industry terminology and aroma classification can be checked against GB/T 15109, while the specific product standard and grade are governed by the bottle label.

The producer, product name, aroma type, strength, net volume, raw materials, standard and anti-counterfeit information on the label are the basic identity checks. Buy through regular channels and verify the anti-counterfeit mark and batch rather than judging by packaging colour or price alone.

Aroma and tasting

The nose is led by pit aroma, grain aroma and aged aroma. The entry is mellow and sweet, the body is fairly full, the middle opens and the finish is clean. At 52% ABV the aroma releases more directly and the alcohol is more noticeable; 42% and 38% ABV are softer and better suited to slow drinking with food. Strength changes the entry and aroma presentation, but it is not a quality ranking.

For Shede Zhihui, taste in a small glass: smell the resting spirit first, then swirl gently and smell again. Let the liquid stay in the mouth and follow the order of sweet, sour, bitter and umami, along with body weight. Adding water lowers the alcohol and opens the aroma, but it also changes the balance, so add drop by drop and compare.

History and market

The Shede brand was launched in 2001, Zhihui Shede followed in March 2018, and the brand introduced the "Chinese wisdom" slogan that year. After the 2019 old-liquor strategy, Zhihui Shede became an important lever in the company's high-end price band.

The product sits where a regional famous liquor is extending into the national high-end market. Prices move with vintage, channel and supply and demand, and this page gives no figures. Treating price as proof of quality, or the "eight-year base spirit" as a bottle-age promise, are common misreadings.

Buying and storage

For Shede Zhihui, buy through regular channels and check the anti-counterfeit mark, producer and batch. Gift boxes and channel-specific editions should be checked against the label, so that a packaging difference is not mistaken for a difference in the liquid. For a full case, also inspect the seal and storage conditions.

For Shede Zhihui, unopened baijiu should be kept upright, away from light, in a cool place without strong odours. Once opened, drink it reasonably soon; prolonged exposure to air costs aroma. A home cellar cannot reproduce distillery storage conditions, so the year of purchase should not be treated as an automatic quality gain.

Common misconceptions

First, treating "wisdom" as a quality grade. It is a series name and cultural statement. Second, treating "eight-year base spirit" as the age of every drop. It is a company statement about base spirit. Third, treating the old-liquor strategy as a statutory vintage standard. It is a company strategy. Fourth, assuming 52% ABV must be better than 38% ABV. Strength serves product design and drinking context. Fifth, judging authenticity by price or packaging. Anti-counterfeit marks, channels and labels are more reliable.

For Shede Zhihui, begin with the base and the stated strength. Then look at the sweetness or bitterness, and finally ask how it is served. The answers point to different choices, not to a single score.

Shede's style and Baijiu's region and vintage form a useful pair: the contrast shows where one ends and the other begins.
