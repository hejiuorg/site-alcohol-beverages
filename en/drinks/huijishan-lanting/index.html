Kuaijishan Lanting

Kuaijishan's premium Shaoxing yellow-rice wine line, sold as Lanting Xu, Lanting Guochao, Lanting Guoju and Lanting Dashi at different prices; made in Hutong, Keqiao from glutinous rice, wheat qu and Jian Lake water, mostly semi-dry at around 14% ABV, with vintage wording governed by the weighted-average age rule in GB/T 17946.

Kuaijishan Lanting is the premium line that Kuaijishan uses to reach the top of the Shaoxing market, made by Kuaijishan Shaoxing Wine Co., Ltd.. It is not one fixed bottling. Under the name sit Lanting Xu, Lanting Guochao, Lanting Guoju and Lanting Dashi, sold at different prices and with different age statements. What they share is a Shaoxing origin, a base of glutinous rice and wheat qu, longer ageing than the everyday range and heavier packaging. Treating them as one wine is the first assumption to drop.

A product line, not a grade

"Lanting" borrows the name of the famous orchid pavilion in Shaoxing and the literary gathering once held there. GB/T 17946 knows only four sugar-based classes of Shaoxing wine: yuanhong, jiafan (huadiao), shanniang and xiangxue. There is no Lanting grade. Most Lanting bottlings fall inside the jiafan (huadiao) class, with total sugar of 15.1–40.0 g/L, which makes them semi-dry; a few are made dry. Read the sweetness type on the label first, then the series name. The order matters.

The 1743 workshop and the modern company

The company's public report traces the business to 1743, when Zhou Jiamu opened the Yunji workshop in Dongpu, Shaoxing. The workshop won a medal at the 1915 Panama-Pacific International Exposition. In 1951 it was taken over by the new government and renamed the state-run Yunji distillery; in 1967 it became Shaoxing Dongfeng Distillery; in 2007 it took its present corporate name; on 25 August 2014 it listed in Shanghai under code 601579; in December 2022 Zhongjianxin became the controlling shareholder. The registered address today is 2579 Yangshao Road, Hutong Subdistrict, Keqiao District. The 1743 workshop, the legal entity and the current plant are three different things, and they should not be flattened into a single "283-year-old factory" slogan.

Two national standards, one producing region

Kuaijishan Shaoxing Wine Co., Ltd. is named among the drafting organisations of GB/T 17946-2008, alongside the Shaoxing quality supervision bureau, the national yellow-rice-wine quality inspection centre, China Shaoxing Yellow Wine Group, Zhejiang Taipai Shaoxing Wine Factory and the China National Research Institute of Food and Fermentation Industries. The company's own report also says it took part in drafting the national standards for huangjiu and for Shaoxing wine. GB/T 17946 is stricter than GB/T 13662: it sets higher alcohol and non-sugar solids figures and adds a volatile-ester requirement and process rules intended to capture the region's style. A standard defines a production zone; it does not certify an individual bottle.

Jian Lake water, glutinous rice and wheat qu

The standard's definition is narrow: Shaoxing wine must be fermented from high-quality glutinous rice, wheat and water from Jian Lake inside the protected area, using the region's traditional process. Wheat is made into wheat qu, which supplies enzymes and part of the aroma; a starter culture sets fermentation going. Rice starch is first saccharified into fermentable sugar and then converted by fermentation into alcohol and aroma compounds. Ratios, steeping time, steaming and the timing of the stirring step known as kaipa all change the finished wine.

What "228 steps" can and cannot tell you

The company says each drop of Lanting passes through 228 production steps, under a process it calls "15179", with national-level masters and inheritors of nine core craft steps supervising. That is the producer's own account. It is not a grading system in the national standard, which specifies raw materials, traditional process and analytical limits but does not count steps. Taipai, a neighbour in the same city, describes its Benjiu as having 33 steps. The two numbers use different counting methods and cannot be ranked against each other. A step count can describe management complexity; it cannot prove that a wine tastes better.

How a 20-year label is calculated

GB/T 17946 defines "wine age" as the time a base wine spends ageing in jars, vats, tanks or pools after fermentation. It also states that the age printed on a retail package is the weighted average age of the blend. The standard requires a wine labelled three years or older to reach the superior-quality grade, and it requires the weighted average age to be at least the declared age, with the base wine carrying the declared age making up no less than 50%vol. The 20-year and 30-year figures on Lanting Guoju and Lanting Dashi should be read inside that rule, not as a promise that every drop is that old.

The house style: aged aroma, weight and acidity

Lanting's centre of gravity is aged aroma. A clay jar breathes slowly, so the wine oxidises and esterifies over years, developing dried-fruit, wood and savoury notes. Semi-dry sugar gives the palate weight, while acidity keeps it from turning cloying. The contrast with Taipai Benjiu is sharp: Benjiu is deliberately younger, adds no caramel colour and sells freshness and rice aroma. Taipai Handmade Winter Brew takes a third route, using a long, cool fermentation to build a rounder, sweeter body. All three are Shaoxing wine, but their flavour coordinates do not overlap.

At the table: braised food and river fish

Aged aroma and acidity suit rich, dark sauces: red-braised pork, braised duck, soy-braised pork and drunken chicken all benefit from the cut of acidity. River fish, especially hairy crab, works with the semi-dry sweetness and savoury depth. Warm the wine gently in a water bath; boiling drives off the aroma. Lanting is not a replacement for the everyday Kuaijishan Chunzheng 5. The latter belongs on a family table; Lanting is built for banquets and gifting, with a heavier body and a much higher price.

The price ladder: RMB 299 to RMB 2,299

A 2026 channel survey divides Lanting into four rungs: Lanting Xu at about RMB 299, Lanting Guochao at about RMB 699, Lanting Guoju at about RMB 1000 and Lanting Dashi at about RMB 2,299. The 1743 flagship sits far below at about RMB 45, giving the company a ladder from everyday drinking to the top. In the Shaoxing market in 2026, the Lanting invoice price reportedly rose from RMB 310 to RMB 360, with distributors supplying retailers at about RMB 400 and transactions landing near RMB 420, leaving roughly 30% channel margin. Prices move with region, channel and promotion; they are not a quality scale.

Lanting, Benjiu and winter brew side by side

Put the three pages together and the division of labour is clear. Lanting means aged aroma, built through jar ageing and blending. Benjiu means freshness, built by adding no caramel colour and keeping the wine younger. Handmade winter brew means roundness, built by a slow, cool fermentation from the start of winter to the start of spring. The first suits slow drinking and banquets, the second steamed and blanched food, the third red-braised dishes, braises and hot pot. Choose the wrong context and even an expensive bottle will feel out of place.

Correcting "20 years means every drop is 20 years old"

This is the most common misreading of Lanting. Under GB/T 17946, the declared age is the weighted average age of the blend, and the base wine carrying that age must account for at least 50%vol. The standard does not require every drop to reach the declared year. A related myth is that older is always better. The standard only says a wine aged three years or more should reach the superior grade; bottled and jarred wine has a shelf life of at least one year, and every aged wine has a drinking window. Beyond it, oxidation takes over.

Buying and keeping

Check the producer and origin first, then the series, sweetness type, alcohol by volume and net content. Lanting bottlings are commonly around 14% ABV and mostly semi-dry; gift boxes and standard packs differ mainly in packaging and channel, not in grade. Store unopened bottles upright, away from light and heat. Once opened, refrigerate and finish the bottle promptly. Huangjiu is a broad family, and China makes it in many regions; Shaoxing wine is one protected branch of it. Lanting is not the answer to every question about yellow-rice wine.

Put Yellow wine's style next to Kuaijishan Shaoxing Rice Wine Co., Ltd.'s region and vintage; the difference marks the boundary.
