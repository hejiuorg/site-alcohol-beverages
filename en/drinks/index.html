Taipai Handmade Winter Brew

Taipai's handmade Shaoxing series brewed in the cold window from Lidong to Lichun, sold in three-, five-, six-, eight- and ten-year tiers; made in Hutong, Keqiao from glutinous rice, wheat qu and Jian Lake water, pitched at 26–30 °C and fermented for about 70–80 days, with a rounder, sweeter house style.

Pauillac

A communal red wine appellation in northern Médoc, Bordeaux, France, recognised on 14 November 1936, with about 1,200 hectares of vines and 18 classified growths in the 1855 classification, including the first growths Lafite, Latour and Mouton; Cabernet Sauvignon dominates on deep gravel croupes, with a 57 hl/ha base yield and 11% minimum natural alcohol.
