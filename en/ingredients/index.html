IngredientsReviewedEN

Agave

Agave is a succulent genus whose heart, or piña, is rich in inulin-type fructans; after cooking and hydrolysis it is fermented and distilled into tequila and mezcal, with species, maturity and roasting method shaping flavour.

On this page · 9Read IngredientsReviewedEN

Apple

The apple is the fruit of Malus domestica in the rose family, and the combination of sugar, acid and tannin defines cider style; cider apples are grouped as sweet, sharp, bittersweet and bittersharp by acidity and tannin, and the fruit can also be distilled into apple brandy and Calvados.

Barley

An annual cereal of the genus Hordeum; after malting it becomes the base material for beer and whisky, and in baijiu daqu it is often paired with wheat and peas.

Buckwheat

Buckwheat is a pseudocereal in the Polygonaceae family and contains no gluten; it is used in buckwheat beer, soba shochu and some regional baijiu, bringing earthy, honeyed and grainy notes, while its protein and starch require separate adjustments in saccharification and fermentation.

On this page · 8Read IngredientsReviewedEN

Cherry

Cherry is a Prunus fruit, with sweet and sour types; sour cherry (Prunus cerasus) is high in acid and aroma and central to Black Forest cake, kirsch and cherry brandy.

On this page · 8Read IngredientsReviewedEN

Glutinous rice

A rice type whose starch is almost entirely amylopectin, becoming very sticky and soft after cooking; it is a typical raw material for yellow wine and some sweet rice drinks, and often appears in multi-grain baijiu recipes.

Highland Barley

Naked barley of the Tibetan Plateau, cold- and drought-tolerant and early-ripening, the base of tsampa, barley wine and everyday diet in Tibetan areas; high fibre, beta-glucan and a low glycaemic profile have made it a health grain in recent years.

On this page · 8Read IngredientsReviewedEN

Honey

Honey is the sweet substance bees make from nectar, dominated by glucose and fructose; it is the sole sugar source of mead and can add aroma to beer, liqueurs and spirits, but low nitrogen, high sugar and low pH make fermentation hard to start.

On this page · 9Read IngredientsReviewedEN

Hops

Hops are the female flower cones of a perennial climbing plant in the Cannabaceae family; alpha acids isomerise during the boil to give bitterness, essential oils give aroma, and polyphenols and antibacterial compounds affect foam, clarity and shelf life.

On this page · 9Read IngredientsReviewedEN

Juniper

Juniper berries are the female cones of Juniperus communis and rich in terpenes such as alpha-pinene and myrcene; they are the legally required core flavour of gin, and distillation method and dose decide whether juniper leads or blends with other botanicals.

On this page · 8Read IngredientsReviewedEN

Maize

An annual cereal of the genus Zea with high starch content and fat concentrated in the germ; it is a common ingredient in multi-grain baijiu and a core raw material in American bourbon and some beer adjuncts.

On this page · 9Read IngredientsReviewedEN

Millet

Millet is the hulled grain of foxtail millet (Setaria italica) and a general name for small-seeded cereals such as proso and pearl millet; in China it is used in huangjiu and millet wine, and in Africa and Asia for traditional cloudy grain beers, with starch and protein shaping saccharification and flavour.

On this page · 9Read IngredientsReviewedEN

Molasses

Molasses is the syrup left after sugar crystallisation, containing sucrose, glucose, fructose and minerals; it is the main sugar source for rum and a common industrial yeast medium, and its grade and composition shape fermentation speed and flavour.

On this page · 8Read IngredientsReviewedEN

Oats

Oats are an annual cereal of the genus Avena with high fat and beta-glucan content; they bring silky body and haze to beer and are traditional in oatmeal stout, but fat and viscosity make saccharification, lautering and distillation harder than with barley.

On this page · 8Read IngredientsReviewedEN

Pear

Pear is the fruit of the genus Pyrus, eaten fresh and also fermented; specialist perry pears have high tannin, acidity and aroma, and are made into perry in England and Normandy, lighter than apple cider but related in style.

On this page · 8Read IngredientsReviewedEN

Plum

Plum is a Prunus fruit; East Asian “ume” (Prunus mume) differs from Western plums, with high acidity and clean aroma, used for pickles, umeshu and plum vinegar, a shared food tradition in Japan, China and Korea.

On this page · 8Read IngredientsReviewedEN

Potato

Potato is a tuber of the nightshade family, rich in starch and water, and the core material for potato vodka in Poland, Germany, Sweden and Eastern Europe; it is usually rounder and slightly sweeter than grain vodka and naturally gluten-free.

On this page · 8Read IngredientsReviewedEN

Rice

The grain left after rice is husked and milled, and an important starch source for baijiu, yellow wine and sake; japonica and indica rice differ in starch structure, stickiness and steaming behaviour, which directly affect saccharification and fermentation.

On this page · 8Read IngredientsReviewedEN

Rye

Rye is an annual cereal of the genus Secale, hardy and tolerant of poor soil; it is the required base of American rye whiskey and brings a spicy, dry grain character to beer, while beta-glucans and proteins make saccharification and lautering harder.

On this page · 9Read IngredientsReviewedEN

Sorghum

An annual cereal of the genus Sorghum and one of the main grain ingredients in Chinese baijiu; the balance of starch, protein, fat and tannin in the grain shapes fermentation and flavour.

On this page · 9Read IngredientsReviewedEN

Sugarcane

Sugarcane is a tall grass of the genus Saccharum whose stems store sucrose; pressing the juice and fermenting it directly yields cane-juice rum and Brazilian cane spirit, while refining sugar first and fermenting the molasses gives the other major rum route.

On this page · 9Read IngredientsReviewedEN

Water

The most easily overlooked yet ubiquitous ingredient in brewing; its minerals, hardness and pH affect saccharification, fermentation, microbial activity and final flavour, and have long shaped where distilleries and breweries are sited.

On this page · 8Read IngredientsReviewedEN

Wheat

An annual cereal of the genus Triticum, used as a staple food and as a key ingredient in qu making, multi-grain baijiu, beer and whisky; the balance of protein and starch shapes both qu quality and the body of the drink.

On this page · 9Read IngredientsReviewedEN

Yeast

Yeast is a single-celled fungus that turns sugar into ethanol and carbon dioxide and produces esters, higher alcohols and other flavour compounds; strain, pitching method and temperature steer fermentation, making yeast the most important and most overlooked variable in brewing.
